Introduction

This post explores homeless shelter locations in Vancouver using Python. I will examine the shelter data, summarize some of its characteristics, and create a visualization of the results.

About the Dataset

This dataset is from the City of Vancouver Open Data Portal. It contains information about homeless shelter locations in the City of Vancouver. I will use this dataset to create summaries and visualizations.

Dataset: Homeless Shelter Locations
Publisher: City of Vancouver
Data Owner: City of Vancouver
Licence: Open Government Licence - Vancouver
Source: City of Vancouver Open Data Portal

NoteSummary of Findings

The analysis below summarizes the homeless shelter locations in Vancouver using tables and a graph. Some key findings are:

  1. Most shelters serve adults of all genders, with 12 out of 17 shelters in this category.
  2. Most shelters do not allow pets. Only 4 out of 17 shelters allow pets.
  3. Carts are allowed at only 2 out of 17 shelters.
  4. The shelters are located across several Geo Local Areas in Vancouver, with the largest number located in Downtown.

The tables below summarize shelter categories, meal support, pets, and carts. The final graph shows the number of shelters by Geo Local Area.
